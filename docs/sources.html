Showing our work

How we made this page

This is the short version. The full explanation covers every step in detail.

Finding facts. We used Tavily, an AI search tool, to find government reports, research papers, news stories, and company announcements. For important facts, we pulled up the full original page and checked the exact wording. Each source on this page is marked "via Tavily."

Following companies. We used Sayari to look up companies by their official registration numbers, see who they ship to and buy from, and see who owns them. We didn't trust searches by company name alone, because they mixed up different companies. Each source from there is marked "via Sayari."

Our rules. We don't call any company "good" or "bad." When someone makes an accusation, we say who made it. When a shipping record doesn't show what was shipped, we say so. Guesses about the future are labeled as estimates.

Use of AI. This page was built and written with help from Claude, an AI assistant. Every fact links to a real source.
