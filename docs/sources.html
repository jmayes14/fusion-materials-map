How this site was made

This is the short version. The full explanation covers every step in detail.

Finding facts. Tavily, an AI search tool, found the government reports, research papers, news stories, and company announcements. For important facts, the full original page was checked for exact wording. Each source on this page is marked "via Tavily."

Following companies. Sayari was used to look up companies by their official registration numbers, see who they ship to and buy from, and see who owns them. Searches by company name alone mixed up different companies, so they weren't used. Each source from there is marked "via Sayari."

Rules. No company is called "good" or "bad." Every accusation names who made it. When a shipping record doesn't show what was shipped, the site says so. Guesses about the future are labeled as estimates.

Use of AI. This page was built and written with help from Claude, an AI assistant. Every fact links to a real source.
