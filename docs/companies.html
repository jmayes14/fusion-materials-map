Following the companies

What company records show

We used Sayari, a database of company registrations, shipping records, government contracts, and sanctions lists. It shows which real companies do business with each other. A trade record proves two companies traded, but not that anything ended up in a fusion plant. Sayari links need a Sayari account to open.

What the records couldn't show: who owns NCCP, exactly what was in each shipment to Isoflex USA, and which buyers of Chilean company SQM take lithium rather than fertilizer. We don't use Sayari's own risk scores. We only report government lists and official records.
